How this works

Every cable is built for a specific signal — some need a shield and two hot conductors to reject hum, some carry raw current, some carry pure data. The connector is just the plug that signal is standardized on. Start matching to find out which is which.

FULL SIGNAL CHAIN — all 10 matched

Every cable found the connector built for its signal. That's the backbone of every rig in the studio.

Some connectors are decoys — a connector's shape doesn't guarantee it's carrying the right signal.

Same shape, different signal: XLR carries a mic or AES digital audio. RCA carries unbalanced line-level or S/PDIF digital. The connector never tells you the whole story.
