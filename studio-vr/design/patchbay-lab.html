01 · Signal Routing

The Telephone Switchboard

How did old-school phone operators connect a caller in New York to a home in Chicago? They plugged a cable directly into a board. Try it yourself — drag from the Synthesizer jack to the Reverb Unit jack and watch what happens to its output.

The take-away

A patch bay is just a central control hub. It brings the hidden back-panel plugs of every piece of studio gear to one easy front panel, so you can re-route audio in seconds without crawling behind a desk.
