Construction Detail

Floating Floor — Decoupling the Room

Both rooms sit on a floor that isn't directly bolted to the building's structure. That gap is deliberate — it's what keeps footsteps, HVAC rumble, and the building itself from bleeding into a recording, and keeps monitor bass from rattling the floor below.

  • 1Structural slab — the building's own floor, left untouched and never fastened directly to anything above it.
  • 2Neoprene isolation pucks — resilient pads spaced across the slab that absorb vibration instead of transmitting it.
  • 3Floating plywood deck — rests on the pucks, structurally independent from the slab below.
  • 4Finished surface — the visible floor, carrying the room's actual foot traffic and furniture load.
